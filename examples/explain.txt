Picture a very large video rental store, tens of thousands of discs on the shelves. The clerk remembers what you took home last time, and which one you gave up on ten minutes in, so by the time you walk in there is already a cart waiting for you.

This system does the clerk's job. It narrows tens of thousands of titles down to a few hundred you might like, puts those in order, and turns them into the rows on your home screen.

Every time you click one or scroll past one, it reports back. Overnight it takes all of that in, and picks a little better tomorrow.
